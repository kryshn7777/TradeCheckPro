Controls

  • 1 Left thumbDrag anywhere on the left half to move and swing. The stick appears under your thumb.
  • 1 Push up / downClimb the tether / let it out. Flick up quickly to jump.
  • 2 Touch an anchorFires the tether at the anchor nearest your finger. Hold to stay attached, lift to let go.
  • 3 Second fingerWhile swinging, touch another anchor to chain straight to it.
  • 4 Pause buttonTop-right corner. The game also pauses when you leave the app.

Prefer a right-hand aim stick, or tap-to-attach instead of holding? Switch it in Settings › Touch.

  • MouseAim. Anchors near the cursor are highlighted.
  • Left buttonHold to fire and keep the tether. Release to let go and keep your momentum.
  • ADMove, and pump your swing while attached.
  • WClimb the tether. Jump when standing.
  • SLet the tether out. Brake on the ground.
  • ←↑→↓ + SpaceKeyboard-only aim: pick a direction, hold Space to tether.
  • EscPPause.
  • RRestart the run instantly.
  • SpaceEnterContinue after a fall.
  • Left stickMove and swing. Up / down climbs or lets out the tether.
  • Right stickAim the tether.
  • RTRBXHold to fire and keep the tether. Release to let go.
  • AJump (bottom face button).
  • StartPause.
  • BackRestart the run.

Button names follow the common layout; face buttons are matched by position.

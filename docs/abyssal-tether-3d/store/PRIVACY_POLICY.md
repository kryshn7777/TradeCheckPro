# Abyssal Tether: Privacy Policy

Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

This is the privacy policy for **Abyssal Tether** ("the game"), a virtual-reality game by {{STUDIO}} for Meta Quest,
also playable in a web browser at https://{{DOMAIN}}{{GAME_PATH}}. It explains what the game stores, why, and how you
can delete it. We wrote it to be short because the game collects very little.

## Who we are

{{STUDIO}}, {{POSTAL_ADDRESS}}. Contact: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

## The short version

- The game does not collect personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party
  tracking code.
- Your settings, best times, progress and a cached copy of the game stay **on your headset** (in the app's own
  browser storage). We never receive them.
- You can delete all of it at any time (see "Deleting your data").

## What the game stores on your device

| What | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Settings (volume, comfort and control options, player height) | to remember your choices | app storage on your device |
| Records and progress (best times, deepest descent, deaths, the checkpoint you can continue from, whether you finished the chapter) | to show your records and let you continue | app storage on your device |
| A copy of the game files | so the game starts quickly and works offline | app cache on your device |

None of this contains your name, email address, Meta account details, location, voice, camera images, body tracking or
any other personal data. The game does not use the microphone or the camera.

## What we collect on our servers

Nothing about you. The game files are served by our hosting provider ({{HOST_NAME}}), which, like any web host,
records standard request logs (IP address, time, requested file, user agent) for security and delivery. These logs are
kept for {{LOG_RETENTION_DAYS}} days and are not used to identify or profile players.

## Meta and the Meta Horizon Store

If you get the game from the Meta Horizon Store, Meta provides the store, your account and (in future versions) any
purchase. Meta's own privacy policy applies to what Meta collects. The game does not read your Meta account data.

## How the information is used

Only to run the game: to remember your settings and progress and to start the game offline. We do not sell, share or
use it for advertising.

## Children

The game is rated for teens and adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect
information from children.

## Deleting your data

Because the data is only on your device, you control it:

- On your headset: **Settings > Apps > Abyssal Tether > Clear data** (or uninstall the game). This removes your
  settings, records, progress and the cached game files.
- In a web browser: clear the site's data for {{DOMAIN}} in the browser's settings.
- If you think we hold anything about you, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} and we will check and delete it. We do not hold
  account data, so there is normally nothing to delete.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or
port your personal data, or to object to its use. Because we do not collect personal data, there is normally nothing
to access or delete, but you can write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} and we will respond within 30 days.

## Changes

If the game's data practices change (for example when paid content is added), we will update this page and the
effective date before the change ships.
